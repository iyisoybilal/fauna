Common questions

The deterrent sound doesn't seem to affect the animal.

Effect varies by animal, age, and hearing. High-frequency sounds work on many dogs and cats but never on every individual, and they fade with distance. Never rely on sound alone around an aggressive animal.

I bought Fauna Pro but a mode is still locked.

Open any locked mode and tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and restore for free on any device.

Does Smart Auto-Calm record me?

No. It listens on your device only to detect a bark or meow, and plays a calming sound automatically. Nothing is ever recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Is there a subscription?

No. Fauna Pro is a single one-time purchase — yours forever, no ads.

Where are the bird calming sounds?

Bird calming has weak scientific evidence, so we deliberately left it out rather than ship something that doesn't work. Bird deterrence uses real predator calls.
